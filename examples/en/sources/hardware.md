# The corner hardware store (facts to work with)

- Name: Ferris Supply, on the corner of Ninth and Alder, in business since 1961.
- The current owner, Ruth Ferris, is the granddaughter of the founder and runs the counter herself.
- The store still cuts keys by hand and threads pipe in the back room.
- Screws are sold out of wooden drawers, not packaged; each drawer has a hand-inked label.
- Ruth keeps a ledger of house accounts for a dozen longtime customers instead of taking deposits.
- A shop cat named Mortar sleeps on the seed rack and has never caught anything.
- The store closes at 5:30 pm sharp; Ruth flips the sign while still talking to whoever is inside.
- On Saturdays her nephew works the register and gets every price lookup wrong.
