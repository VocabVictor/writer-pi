# Writing Brief

- Target language: English (the entire piece must be in English, with no words from other languages except quotations)
- Form: neighborhood essay
- Subject: the corner hardware store near my apartment
- Length: about 300 words (250-400 words)
- Tone: plain and concrete; write details the narrator has actually seen, no vague sentiment
- Banned words: journey, vibrant, nestled
- Other constraints: do not open with a line about how the neighborhood is changing; end without a moral
